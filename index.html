03 · About

Design eye, system brain

I just finished a bachelor in Information Systems at Østfold University College, where I found my place between code and design: I like to sketch an idea in Figma or Illustrator and then build it for real.

Before my studies I spent many years in logistics and customer service, and I started out in IT support at IBM. That taught me to work with all kinds of people, take responsibility and keep things running, which I bring to every team I join.

In my bachelor thesis I led the front-end for a planning tool for Nexans, and in group projects I often end up holding the threads together: meeting minutes, direction and getting things shipped.
